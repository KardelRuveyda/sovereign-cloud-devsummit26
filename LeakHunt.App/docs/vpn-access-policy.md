# VPN Access Policy (SYNTHETIC DATA, internal)

All remote access to production requires the corporate VPN and multi-factor authentication.
Contractors get time-boxed access of at most 30 days and must be sponsored by an employee.
Privileged access uses just-in-time elevation through PIM with a maximum of 8 hours.
Access reviews run every quarter. Unused accounts are disabled after 45 days.
