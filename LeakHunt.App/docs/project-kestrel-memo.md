# Project Kestrel: Acquisition Memo (SYNTHETIC DATA, board confidential)

Contoso Corp intends to acquire Fabrikam Logistics for EUR 48 million in cash, signing planned for 14 November. Exclusivity runs until 30 November.

Key terms:
- Purchase price: EUR 48M, with an earn-out of up to EUR 6M tied to 2027 revenue.
- Fabrikam's top customer represents 31 percent of revenue and has a change-of-control clause.
- Walk-away price approved by the board: EUR 53M.
